Apex Poker — Terms & Rules

Last updated: September 2026

1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or play on Apex Poker. By registering, you confirm that you meet this age requirement and that it is lawful for you to participate in real-money poker in your jurisdiction. It is your responsibility to check local laws before depositing or playing.

2. Chips and Real Money

Chips used at the tables represent value backed by real money deposits made directly to the operator. Chips have no cash value outside this platform and cannot be transferred between accounts. All balances are recorded in your account ledger and may be reviewed by the operator at any time.

3. Deposits

To deposit, you must first send a real bank transfer to the operator using the payment details provided. After sending the transfer, submit a deposit request in the app with the amount and a reference note. Your chips will be credited only after the operator manually confirms the transfer has arrived in the operator's bank account. Submitting a deposit request does not guarantee approval — false or unverifiable requests will be rejected and may result in account suspension.

4. Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests are processed at the operator's discretion and paid out via bank transfer to the account holder's verified details. Processing times may vary. The operator reserves the right to request identity verification before releasing a withdrawal.

5. Fair Play

Collusion between players, use of bots or automated assistance, multiple accounts by the same person, and any form of chip dumping or intentional loss to another account are strictly prohibited. Violations may result in forfeiture of chip balance and permanent account closure.

6. Responsible Gaming

Poker involves financial risk. Only play with money you can afford to lose. If you feel your play is becoming compulsive or is affecting your finances, relationships, or wellbeing, stop playing and seek support. Minors are strictly prohibited from using this platform.

7. Account Suspension & Termination

The operator may suspend or close any account suspected of fraud, collusion, chargebacks, abusive behavior, or violation of these terms, and may withhold associated chip balances pending investigation.

8. Disputes

Any dispute about a hand result, chip balance, deposit, or withdrawal should be reported to the operator directly. The operator's decision on disputed matters is final. Apex Poker is provided on an "as is" basis with no guarantee of uninterrupted availability.

9. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

10. Contact

For questions, disputes, or support, contact the operator directly through the app.
